# Names and logo

**Draft. This policy has not been reviewed by counsel and will change.**

## What the licences cover, and what they do not

Arynwood Cutroom's source code is free software under the GNU General Public License
(version 3 only, like the Kdenlive code it is built on). You may use, change and share
that code under the terms of the licence, and no permission from Arynwood is needed for that.

The GPL covers the code. It does not give anyone the right to use Arynwood's names or logo.

## What is reserved

- The names **Arynwood** and **Arynwood Cutroom** (and **Cutroom** as the name of this
  program).
- The **Arynwood tree logo** and the splash image (`data/pics/arynwood-logo.png`,
  `data/pics/arynwood-splash.png`), which are artwork by Lorelei Noble and are not under the
  GPL. See `LICENSES/LicenseRef-Arynwood-Logo.txt`.

## What that means in practice

- **Official builds** published by Arynwood Technology may carry the name and logo.
- **A modified or rebuilt version that you distribute** should not be called Arynwood
  Cutroom, should not use the tree logo, and should not suggest that Arynwood made or endorses
  it. Give it its own name and its own icon. Keep the licence notices and the credits to
  Kdenlive, D-Ogi and Arynwood that the source contains.
- **Saying what it is built from is fine.** "Based on Arynwood Cutroom" or "a fork of
  Arynwood Cutroom" is a plain description and needs no permission.
- **A build for your own use**, or for testing, is not covered by the rule above.

## Kdenlive

Kdenlive is a project of the Kdenlive team and KDE. Arynwood Cutroom is a modified version
of Kdenlive, is not made or endorsed by either, and uses the name Kdenlive only to say what
it is based on. Please report problems with Cutroom to Arynwood, not to KDE.
